Trivia1v1 — export app icons

This page renders the assets/icon.svg "VS duel shield" logo at the exact sizes the Android APK build needs, then downloads them straight into your Downloads folder. Move the files to assets/ (the manifest already references them) and you're ready to build.

Why maskable? Android crops launcher icons into circles/squircles. The maskable icon has a full-bleed background with the logo inside the central ~66% safe zone, so nothing gets chopped. The 192/512 versions keep the rounded tile for anything that still uses a classic square icon.
