Storyboard — 15 seconds, hook first

      
0:00–0:02
Hook. On-screen: "You still think honey expires? 🍯" (big bold text, orange bg). Audio: energetic voiceover or trending sound.

      
0:02–0:07
Fact #1. Slide: honey never spoils — 3,000-year-old honey from Egyptian tombs is still edible. On-screen: "HONEY NEVER SPOILS 🍯".

      
0:07–0:12

0:12–0:15
CTA. Slide: "PROVE IT." + Play TriviaDuel pill. Voiceover: "Think you know trivia? Prove it — free, link in bio."

      STRATEGY
      
💡 Posting tips

    


    

      
    
        
  • Post in order: upload slides 1→7 (or 1→12) exactly as numbered. Slide 1 is your cover — it does the scrolling for you.
    • 
        
  • Post the fun-facts carousel on weekdays (Mon/Wed/Fri) and the quiz on weekends — quizzes get more saves because people come back to test friends.
    • 
        
  • Rotate quiz topics weekly — Geography one week, General Mix the next, Classic after that. Regulars stay hooked when the questions keep changing.
    • 
        
  • Best times: 7–9pm local time on weekdays; 10am–12pm on weekends.
    • 
        
  • End each post with a question ("Which fact surprised you? / What's your score?") — comments push the post to new feeds.
    • 
        
  • Pin the first comment with the app link (copy above).
    • 
        
  • Stories: share the cover slide with a "Think you know trivia?" poll sticker — 2-option poll drives taps to the carousel.
    • 
        
  • To get PNG files: use the Export buttons (opens a preview — then Download), or open a slide at full size and screenshot it at 100% zoom.
    • 
        
  • Switch up facts weekly — new facts + new quiz every week keeps the series alive.
